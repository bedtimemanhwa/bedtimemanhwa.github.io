About Bedtime Manhwa

Bedtime Manhwa is a small channel of long, spoken recaps of action manhwa with witty narration: the whole story told in order, part by part. Each recap has original narration, editing and sound; the artwork is the creators', shown with credit.

How we treat the creators

  • Every recap, article and page names the artist, writer and original author, as the official release credits them.
  • Every series page links to the official release. If you enjoy a story, please read it there.
  • Articles stay spoiler-light and never run past the last episode the matching video covers.

Where to find us

The recaps live on YouTube: youtube.com/@bedtimemanhwa. The quickest way to reach us is a comment under a video.
