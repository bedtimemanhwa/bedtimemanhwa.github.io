Bad Born Blood recap

An orphan cadet gives the Empire his eye, and never flinches.

The Empire takes children and turns them into weapons. Number thirty-two is Luca, a lower-city orphan in the Imperial Guard, where broken parts get swapped for titanium and loyalty is the only thing that can't be replaced. Set in the RF Online universe, on the planet Novus.
