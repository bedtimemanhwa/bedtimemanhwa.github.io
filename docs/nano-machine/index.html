Nano Machine recap

The Demonic Cult's unwanted son, and the machine from the future living in his blood.

Cheon Yeo-woon is the Demonic Cult's seventh heir and its least wanted one: no martial arts, no allies, and assassins on his heels. Then a descendant from the future injects him with Nano, billions of tiny machines that rebuild his body, copy any technique he sees and never stop talking. Our recap follows his climb through the Cult's academy and the war between the heirs.
