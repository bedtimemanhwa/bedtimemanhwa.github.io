Privacy policy

Last updated: {{ site.legal_updated }}

This page covers this website and Forge, the in-house tool {{ site.title }} uses to upload and manage videos on its own YouTube channel.

This website

This website has no accounts, forms, comments, advertising or analytics. It doesn't set cookies or collect personal information. Embedded YouTube videos are served by YouTube under Google's privacy policy.

Forge and the YouTube API

Forge is used only by the {{ site.title }} team, on the team's own computer, to manage the {{ site.title }} YouTube channel. When the channel owner signs in with Google, Forge asks for access to YouTube through the YouTube Data API so it can:

  • upload our own videos and set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions, playlists and publishing schedule;
  • read the channel's own videos and basic channel details, such as its name and subscriber count.

What Forge does not do:

  • It doesn't access anyone else's channel, viewers' personal data, email, contacts or files.
  • It doesn't sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Nothing from YouTube is used for advertising or for training any model.
  • It doesn't send data anywhere except to YouTube itself.

Forge keeps two things, both on the team's own computer only: its sign-in token, and a log of its own uploads (video titles, video IDs and dates). It uses them only for the actions listed above. Forge's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Its use of YouTube follows the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

The account owner can remove Forge's access at any time on the Google Account page Third-party apps with account access. The token Forge holds stops working immediately. Forge's Disconnect button deletes that token from the computer, and the upload log can be deleted on request.
